Checking certificate…

A certificate is genuine only if its code, the name and the course printed on it match the official register of the centre. If the details above differ from the paper or PDF in front of you, treat the certificate as unverified.

Certificate of Completion: the holder passed the course assessment. Certificate of Attendance: the holder attended the course but did not pass the assessment. Where a grade is shown, it is the grade recorded by the centre.
